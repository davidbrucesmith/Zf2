## 1. Overview

This document locks the procedure for S01.03 as derived from D01.01. No conditions are changed mid-pilot without a logged decision entry in `S01.03_melodyne_stem_pilot_journal.md`.

Note: the DP conforming pass in §4.1 may produce a sufficiently aligned symbolic representation to reduce or eliminate the need for Melodyne extraction on this particular example. The experiment proceeds regardless, as the process validates the workflow and establishes best practice for future material.

## 2. Section Selection Criteria

The 16-measure Melodyne target is identified as an output of the structural fidelity pass (§4.1). The selected passage must satisfy all of the following.

1. Length: 16 target measures with 2-measure handles on both sides.
2. Content: the stem set for the candidate zone must contain identifiable melody, clear bass function, and moderate harmonic density across the available stems.
3. Form: at least one phrase boundary and one cadence within the target window.
4. Texture: no exceptionally dense tutti material across the full stem sum.
5. Metric stability: the candidate zone must show acceptable conformance to the DP grid after §4.1 alignment — no unresolved splits or elisions within the target window.

Having 9 individual stems available (rather than only the stereo mix) is expected to improve Melodyne's note extraction accuracy by reducing polyphonic ambiguity per track. Whether this expectation holds is one of the evaluation questions for this pilot.

Record section metadata (§2.1) at the end of §4.1, before proceeding to §4.2.

## 3. Fixed Conditions

The following are locked for this pilot and may not be varied without a decision log entry.

1. One selected Suno version only. Selection is a curatorial act performed across multiple Suno generation iterations and is governed by one of two criteria:
   - **Fidelity criterion:** the output most closely adheres to the structural, metrical, and motivic framework of the DP transformation.
   - **Discovery criterion:** the output contains something compositionally valuable that emerged from Suno independent of the original transformation.
   Both criteria are valid; the governing criterion for the selected version must be recorded in §2.1 (Selection rationale).
2. Melodyne as the sole transcription engine.
3. Comparison scope:
   - Full DP-positioned mix render
   - Melody stem
   - Bass stem
   - Harmonic/accompaniment stem
4. No aggressive preprocessing beyond level matching and documented trimming.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Structural Fidelity Pass and Section Selection

This phase operates on the full-length Suno result. It aligns the Suno output to the DP MIDI structure, characterizes metrical and hierarchical drift, and identifies the best 16-measure candidate for the Melodyne pilot. This alignment is a permanent step in the iterative workflow — required on every loop cycle to maintain synchrony with the pre-Suno transformation material.

Temporal authority is always Suno: the DP tempo map/conductor track is adjusted to follow the Suno audio's native timing. The Suno audio is never time-stretched to fit the DP grid. The two variants below differ only in how divergent content is handled, not in timing authority.

1. Select target section and handles per §2.
2. Place selected Suno result into DP against the source structure. Export a conductor map at this point (pre-conform baseline).
3. Produce two alignment variants in parallel:

   **Variant A — Cut/elide at divergence:** Where Suno's content diverges from the DP transformation structure, split or elide the Suno track. Preserve the original unedited Suno audio on a separate track. Export a conductor map after adjustment.

   **Variant B — Retain at divergence:** Allow the Suno content to continue through divergent zones without cuts; accept the structural difference. Export a conductor map after adjustment.

4. Compare the two variants. Select the best-performing zones from either for the working version. Document which zones were taken from which variant and why.
5. Bounce a DP-positioned transcription render from the selected working version.
6. Version 2 reintegration (optional): identify any Suno sections with compositional value outside the original transformation and reintegrate where desired. Document which sections were reintegrated.
7. Select the 16-measure Melodyne target from the working version, satisfying all criteria in §2. Complete §2.1 Section Metadata before proceeding.

> **Gate:** Do not proceed to §4.2 until §2.1 is complete and the 16-measure target is confirmed.
