### 3. Interpretation

- **SMF alignment (bar grid snap):** Material landed within beat parameters. Only minor manual adjustments required — no systematic offset or global drift detected.
- **Lead Vocal MIDI quality:** Vocal lines captured with strong melodic contour and expressive pitch detail. Spurious notes present in higher-than-expected quantity, attributable to performance expressivity (vibrato, portamento, microtonal inflection) generating extra blobs in Melodyne. These represent a cost of preserving expressive material rather than random noise — correctable in cleanup without losing compositionally significant content.
- **String Section MIDI quality:** Intonation, key centre, and most rhythmic values captured well. Articulation was imprecise — note boundaries and attack detail require cleanup. Acceptable given that cleanup was anticipated regardless; the harmonic and rhythmic skeleton is present and usable.
- **Unexpected anomalies:** None beyond the spurious vocal notes. No catastrophic failures, missing sections, or octave-register errors noted.

### 4. Comparative Insight

- **What worked:** Grid alignment via SMF tempo map; overall harmonic and rhythmic fidelity of both stems; vocal melodic contour preservation; string section key and rhythm capture.
- **What did not work:** Vocal spurious notes require cleanup pass; string articulation imprecise.
- **Go/no-go for full 9-stem R03:** **Go.** Process is validated. Cleanup burden is acceptable and expected.
